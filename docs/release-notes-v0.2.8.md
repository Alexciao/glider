# Glider v0.2.8

Update now: Glider shows a "Glider is ready" notice once the update has downloaded; choose **Restart**, or it installs when you quit Glider. Or run `Glider-win-Setup.exe` and choose **Update**.

## New

- **Your playtime from the Epic Games Launcher.** A game's playtime now includes the time Epic recorded when you played through its launcher, on top of the time you play in Glider. Glider reads it with the sign-in it already has, refreshes it hourly and after you close a game, and counts every minute once: Epic doesn't record games started from Glider, and if a game ever reports its own time to Epic, Glider notices and doesn't add it twice.
